Privacy Policy

App: Gallery Note  ·  Developer: TLT Data  ·  Last updated: June 2025

Your privacy matters. Gallery Note is built with a privacy-first approach — your original files never leave your device, and we never sell your data or use it to train AI models.

1. Who We Are

Gallery Note is developed and operated by TLT Data. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at support@tltdata.com.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the app's features:

  • Files you process — when you use AI features (auto-tagging, OCR, speech-to-text, translation, color analysis), the relevant file or a portion of it is sent to our processing servers. Your original files are never stored permanently on our servers.
  • Location data (EXIF) — location metadata embedded in your photos is stripped before any file is sent to external services.
  • Notes and tags — stored locally on your device. Not sent to our servers unless required for a specific feature.
  • Usage and diagnostic data — anonymized crash reports and performance data to help us improve the app.
  • Purchase data — managed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We receive only transaction confirmations, not your payment details.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve app features (OCR, tagging, transcription, translation)
  • To process your subscription and in-app purchases
  • To respond to support requests
  • To fix bugs and improve performance

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data to train AI models.

4. Third-Party Services

Gallery Note uses the following third-party services to power certain features. Each service processes data only as needed to perform its function:

  • Google Cloud Vision (OCR) — for text extraction from images
  • Google Gemini — for AI-powered tagging and analysis
  • Microsoft Azure — for speech-to-text transcription and translation
  • Supabase — for secure backend data storage
  • Render — for app server infrastructure

All third-party providers are bound by their respective privacy policies and data processing agreements. Location (EXIF) data is stripped from files before they are sent to any external service.

5. Audio Data

Audio files submitted for transcription are deleted from our servers immediately after processing is complete. We do not retain audio recordings.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your notes, tags, and processed results are stored securely using Supabase. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. On-device processing is used for basic features wherever possible.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data within the app:

  • Delete any data at any time — EXIF, OCR results, tags, transcripts, color analysis, or any combination
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at support@tltdata.com
  • If you are in a jurisdiction with data protection rights (such as GDPR or CCPA), you may also request access to, correction of, or portability of your personal data

8. Children's Privacy

Gallery Note is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
