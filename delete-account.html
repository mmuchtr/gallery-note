Delete Account & Data

App: Gallery Note · Developer: TLT Data · Last updated: June 2026

Gallery Note gives you full control over your data. You can delete specific data (tags, OCR results, notes) without deleting your account, or permanently delete your entire account.

Option 1: Delete Specific Data (Without Deleting Account)

You can delete data attached to individual photos or videos without removing your account:

  1. Open Gallery Note and select a photo or video.
  2. Tap the Delete option.
  3. Choose what to delete:
    • Delete photo/video — removes the media file from your device.
    • Delete data only — removes the associated data (tags, OCR results, notes) while keeping the photo/video.
  4. Confirm your choice when prompted.
Tags, OCR results, and notes can also be individually deleted from within the app at any time.

Option 2: Delete Your Entire Account

  1. Open Gallery Note on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your account profile and credentials
  • All notes, tags, and OCR results stored in the cloud
  • Any other personal data associated with your account

Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately through the App Store or Google Play before deleting your account.

Data Retention

Some data may be retained for a limited period as required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention). This data will be deleted as soon as the retention period expires. Photos and files stored locally on your device are not affected and must be deleted manually.
