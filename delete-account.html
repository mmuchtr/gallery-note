Delete Account

App: Gallery Note · Developer: TLT Data · Last updated: June 2026

You can permanently delete your Gallery Note account and all associated data directly from within the app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Gallery Note app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
Warning: This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your account profile and credentials
  • All notes, tags, and OCR results stored in the cloud
  • Purchase history and subscription records (active subscriptions must be cancelled separately through the App Store or Google Play)
  • Any other personal data associated with your account

Data Retention

Some data may be retained for a limited period as required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention, legal compliance). This data will be deleted as soon as the retention period expires.

Photos and files stored locally on your device are not affected and must be deleted manually.

Cancel Subscription Before Deleting

If you have an active subscription, please cancel it through the App Store or Google Play before deleting your account. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription.

Contact

If you have trouble deleting your account or need assistance, please contact us at support@tltdata.com.
